Atlas Odyssey is a geography game about thinking beyond the first answer that comes to mind.

01

Read the clue

Each clue describes something true about multiple countries. Your job is to find one that fits.

02

Explore the atlas

Click a country on the map or use the search box. You can change your selection until you lock it in.

03

Choose your answer

Submit one country per clue. A correct answer earns points based on its rarity. A miss earns zero.

04

Reach the finish

Complete five clues to see your total distance, rarity tiers, and a breakdown of your journey.
